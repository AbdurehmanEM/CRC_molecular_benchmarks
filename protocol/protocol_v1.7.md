## §2 (amended) — Epithelial compartment correspondence

The original protocol named **HCT116 and SW48** as the chip epithelial cell sources. Both are mismatch-repair-deficient / microsatellite-unstable (MSI) lines. The discovery cohort (GSE178341) contains **34 MMR-deficient and 28 MMR-proficient donors**, and MMR-proficient (MSS) tumours are the majority of colorectal cancer in the population. A chip epithelial compartment built only from MSI lines therefore represents part of the population from which the programs were derived.

**Amended correspondence:**

| Human tissue compartment | Proposed chip cell source |
|---|---|
| Malignant epithelial cells | One MMR-deficient line (**HCT116** or **SW48**) **and** one MMR-proficient line (**SW480** or **HT-29**) |
| Fibroblasts and related stromal states | hMSC (unchanged) |
| Myeloid cells | THP-1-derived macrophages (unchanged) |
| Endothelial cells | HUVEC (unchanged) |

**Rationale for the specific MMR-proficient candidates.** SW480 and HT-29 are both MMR-proficient / chromosomally unstable. They are offered as alternatives rather than fixed, because the choice is a wet-lab design decision and the two carry different driver backgrounds (SW480: APC/KRAS/TP53, the canonical adenoma–carcinoma genotype; HT-29: BRAF V600E, closer to the serrated/CIMP route). Lines were deliberately **not** drawn in same-patient pairs (SW480/SW620, DLD-1/HCT-15), since a pair derived from one patient does not add an independent tumour background. Caco-2 was not proposed, as it is primarily a barrier/differentiation model rather than a tumour model.

**Scope limit — unchanged in substance.** These remain biological correspondences for *proposed future* measurements. No cell-line data were analysed anywhere in this study; the programs and panels are defined entirely from patient tissue. Naming two microsatellite classes widens the proposed representation but does not establish that either line reproduces patient epithelial states, and the pre-existing interpretation limits in Table 2 continue to apply.

**Verification before wet-lab use.** The MMR/MSI status of any line actually used should be confirmed against Cellosaurus / CCLE at the time of purchase, together with STR authentication.
